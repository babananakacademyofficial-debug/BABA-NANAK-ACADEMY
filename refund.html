Refund & Cancellation Policy

Students should review the selected course and fee before confirming payment.

If a payment is completed but enrolment is not confirmed because of a technical issue, the student should contact Baba Nanak Academy with the payment ID and Student ID so the transaction can be checked.

Any eligible refund or cancellation will be handled according to the academy's applicable course and admission rules and the payment gateway transaction status.

Duplicate or failed transactions should be reported with the relevant Razorpay payment reference.
