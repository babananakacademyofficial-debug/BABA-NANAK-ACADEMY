Privacy Policy

Baba Nanak Academy collects student information such as name, email, mobile number, date of birth and address for registration, course enrolment, academic records and certificate services.

Payment processing is handled through Razorpay. Baba Nanak Academy does not store card, UPI or banking credentials on its website. Payment confirmation data may be stored for accounting and enrolment records.

Student information is used for academy services, support, examinations, results, DMC and certificates. We do not sell student personal information.

For questions about your information, contact Baba Nanak Academy through the contact details published by the academy.
